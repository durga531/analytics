# Equity research and portfolio work

The standard is output-standards.md (Equity research standard) and templates.md T10, the current investment committee rules; T11 for deep research on fresh capital. T9 and T12 are earlier engines kept for reference only; their sell-type verdicts are retired.

## Mandate (M2, enforced by the guard)
Verdicts only BUY, ACCUMULATE, HOLD, WATCH or AVOID. No sell, trim, exit, reduce, stop-loss or tax-loss suggestions on existing holdings: not in notes, not in chat, not in prompts written for other tools. Use pause triggers and the thesis-breaker datapoint instead.

## Every analysis
- Quote the policy from T10; do not re-derive it.
- Primary documents over secondary; every decision-driving claim graded F, S, I or ND, with the evidence mix counted; prices carry an IST timestamp; time series of 8+ quarters or 3+ years; flag when a prior call changes.
- Prose at most 1,200 words unless asked; data in tables or CSV blocks; headers of 3 to 4 words; no superlatives without a benchmark.
- The T10 self-audit line before the action cards, then one ACTION CARD per stock with the T10 fields.
- Argue against __OWNER__'s thesis when the evidence does; if anything about a portfolio move is unclear, ask before working.

## Notes
`04-Invest/Theses/<TICKER>.md` from the Thesis template: the T10 table, the latest ACTION CARD, a dated log of calls and what changed, and review_on set to the next thesis-breaker release date.
