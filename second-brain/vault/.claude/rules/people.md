# People notes

- One note per person, named by full name. Frontmatter: type: person, org, role, relation, last_contact, follow_up.
- What they care about, how they like to work, a dated interaction log (newest first) and commitments in both directions.
- Facts and observed preferences, never judgments of character; write as if the person may read it.
- Wording a partner or client has blessed is recorded verbatim (it is final) and linked from the engagement hub.
