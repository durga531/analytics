# /storyline
A signed-off storyline: problem, governing thought, key lines and a titles-only chain that /deck-build can build without guessing. Routing: top tier (recurring ask 2, strategic half).

## Read first
- The latest version of the working deck and its inputs (M6); where __OWNER__ has edited the file, that edit is the base [C1]. Say which file and date you build on.
- __OS_DIR__/output-standards.md: Deck rules (Deck type and scope, Storyline and headers, Language, Numbers and evidence, Process) and Formatting bans (banned and extreme words, headline limits).
- __OS_DIR__/templates.md: T13 (deck intake and cardinal rules); T21 (playbook deck storyline); T25 (working-session deck structure); T19 (argument-first headline chain) as the exemplar; T42 (story step grammar) and T44 (capability deck storyline) for product decks; T47 (directional choices workshop) for workshops; T14 (slide-craft standard and toolchain); T18 (template operating system), build protocol step 1.
- __OS_DIR__/active-areas.md: the area this deck serves (status, good output, rejected) and its agreed architecture and sections.
- __OS_DIR__/thinking-patterns.md: Challenge vs execution (a new playbook and any structural change are challenge zones); Closed debates stay closed.

## Steps
1. Intake with the T13 questions, one or two at a time, skipping any the request or the record already answers. Proceed only once audience, decision and design language are known; when __OWNER__ says infer, infer from the record and state each inference.
2. Define the problem before any slide: one specific, measurable problem statement and the decision the deck must produce, for whom and by when. Get sign-off.
3. Choose the spine: T21 for programme and playbook decks, T25 for co-creation working sessions (no value or return numbers), T42 with T44 for product decks, T47 for workshops. Open at programme level.
4. Write the governing thought, then the key lines (T18 step 1). Draft the executive summary first; each of its lines points to the slide that proves it.
5. Storyboard titles before content: one complete-sentence conclusion per slide under the T13 title rules, one message per slide, each planned as title, insight, evidence and implication (T14) with its exhibit and source. Missing evidence reads "TBD, source required"; never fill a gap with a guess.
6. Headers-only round: read the titles alone, in order, against T19; rewrite until they carry the whole case; show __OWNER__ the titles-only list and wait before anything is built.
7. Partner test every slide and cut what does not earn its place; detailed tables and registers go to the appendix. Check the agreed architecture is intact; if the better story needs a structural change, stop, explain and wait.
8. For a headers-only review of an existing deck, extract the titles in order and run steps 6 and 7 only.

## Rules enforced here
- Headers alone read as one flowing, compelling storyline in sequence; a titles-only read that breaks is rewritten before any build [C1].
- Frame at programme level before any single playbook; no one section's detail crowds out the programme story [A20].
- The executive summary states what we found, what we are building and what each solution fixes against the as-is; it never leads with or commits to a value number [A5].
- Statements are precise, client-credible findings, never slogans or colloquial lines [A4].
- No extreme or absolute words (the list in output-standards.md); use neutral maturity wording. Finalised slides stay as they are unless __OWNER__ reopens them [A13].
- The agreed architecture (levers, house, sections) is never silently broken; for structural change, think and tell first and change only once agreed [A14].
- Client headers carry no exact numbers; use percentages or approximations. Header wording a partner has blessed is used verbatim.
- No term, code or initiative ID appears before the slide that defines it.
- Sources sit in notes or subscript, never in headlines [B2].
- Every slide passes the partner test, justifying its place to a partner reading it cold; the outline is a final deck with no slide on what changed or where content moved [A1].

## Delegate
- Fact gaps and lookups (validate or disprove, with source and date) to runner.
- Initiative design and initiative pages to /initiative.
- An independent check of the titles-only chain to critic (T3 independent review brief: headline rules, cross-slide consistency).
- The agreed outline to /deck-build; slides are never built on this tier.

## Output
- `03-Work/<Engagement>/<Deck> Storyline.md`: problem statement, audience and decision, governing thought, key lines, the numbered titles-only chain, then per slide the exhibit, evidence source and status (to validate, or TBD, source required).
- In chat: the titles-only list for the headers-only round, and any structural change proposed with its reason, awaiting approval.
- Agreed decisions go to the engagement hub (T5 decision and action log).
- M1 no em dashes. M3 final deck only, no version language. M5 never invent a number, source, quote or case; unverifiable claims are cut, not caveated. M6 name the file version you built on.
