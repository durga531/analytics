# /weekly
The week's review in `09-Reviews/YYYY-Www.md`, a refreshed Now.md and decided lessons; in the first review of each month, sourced proposals to update the six OS files. Routing: top tier (recurring ask 27 and the weekly rhythm).

## Read first
- `09-Reviews/Now.md`, last week's review and `09-Reviews/Lessons Queue.md`.
- The week's record, gathered by runner: daily notes (Plan, Log, Close), meeting notes, engagement hubs, review logs and files changed.
- __OS_DIR__/templates.md: T1 (executive answer structure).
- __OS_DIR__/output-standards.md: the verbatim-corrections lists and the section each pending rule targets.
- __OS_DIR__/thinking-patterns.md: Challenge vs execution (the four triggers for a challenge); Closed debates.
- __OS_DIR__/active-areas.md and recurring-asks.md: every Status line, the ranked asks and their routing; both in full for the monthly pass.
- `.claude/rules/journal.md` and `08-Library/Templates/Weekly Review.md`.

## Steps
1. Scorecard: each Now.md must-win with its result (done, partly or missed), the evidence link and the real reason.
2. Where the time went: by area and by recurring-ask number, from notes and file activity, set against the must-wins; name the gap and what caused it.
3. Patterns that recurred: a correction given twice (the rule exists, so name the skill, rule or hook that failed to hold it), asks that came back often enough for a skill or script to absorb, rework loops.
4. Judgement audit: each answer or decision accepted this week without challenge although one of the four triggers was present; say what the challenge would have been and whether it still changes anything. Claude's outputs and __OWNER__'s calls are both in scope; closed debates are not.
5. Lessons queue: for every pending row __OWNER__ decides approve, merge, reject or hold. On approval, append the quote verbatim to the end of the matching verbatim-corrections list (the next number in that list; a new engagement or project starts a new list after the last), add the rule to its target section with its source, set the file's `updated:` date, and mark the row done with its new tag.
6. Next week: at most three must-wins, each finishable within the week and carrying a done-test; deadlines and commitments; what is parked. Write them to Now.md, with a stop-or-park list in the review.
7. Monthly (the first review of the month, or when asked): propose updates to the six files from the month's evidence (area statuses, new corrections, templates, recurring-ask counts and routing), each as file, section, exact text and source. Apply each only on __OWNER__'s approval.

## Rules enforced here
- Descriptive tallies are rejected: every finding states the hypothesis, the root cause and the implication, robust enough to survive a senior challenge; last week's diagnosis is re-examined from first principles, never reused as is [A19].
- Re-read, never recall: the review is built from this week's files as they stand, never from memory of earlier sessions [A16].
- Existing correction tags and T-numbers never move: entries are appended, never inserted, reordered or reworded, so every skill's citations keep their meaning.
- A promoted quote is copied exactly as queued; the rule is as strict as the correction, never softened into advice.
- Closed debates stay closed; no proposal reopens one unless __OWNER__ does.
- Nothing in the six files changes without __OWNER__'s approval of that specific item.

## Delegate
- Gathering to runner: the week's notes with their Close sections, tasks done, open and carried, files changed by folder with versions, and the queue rows; paths and dates only, no judgement.
- The judgement audit's candidate list to critic for an independent read when the week carried a large decision.

## Output
- `09-Reviews/YYYY-Www.md` from the Weekly Review template; Now.md refreshed; queue rows updated; approved items written into the OS files.
- In chat, the T1 shape: the week in one sentence; the logic (scorecard, top pattern, judgement audit finding); the one risk to next week; the so-what as next week's must-wins. Then the approvals waiting, numbered for a one-word reply each.
- M1 no em dashes. M2 any investing line uses the verdict vocabulary only. M4 no proposal changes the locked fact base in T50 (resume architecture and fact base) without a primary source and __OWNER__'s approval. M5 every claim traces to a note or file; no hours, results or sources are invented.
