# Knowledge notes

- One idea per note; the title is the claim. Body: the claim, the evidence, the source (book, page or link), the strongest counter-argument, and the so-what for the work, the portfolio or life.
- Link every new note to at least two existing notes; search before creating to avoid near-duplicates.
- Primary sources and serious scholarship; mark contested history or interpretation as contested; never fabricate a quote (M5).
- Time series and trajectories over single data points when numbers are involved.
