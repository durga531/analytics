# /initiative
Initiative designs that find the true gap, plus page content ready for /deck-build. Routing: top tier (recurring ask 2, strategic half).

## Read first
- The latest version of the deck, the initiative slides named and the raw sources behind them: transcripts, data, documents (M6). Say which version you build on.
- __OS_DIR__/output-standards.md: Deck rules (Numbers and evidence, Design, Language, Process) and Formatting bans.
- __OS_DIR__/templates.md: T7 (initiative refinement method); T22 (initiative page set) and its field spec; T23 (second-wave initiative card); T24 (co-create page); T26 (journey map slide); T27 (persona page); T28 (case study template); T29 (KPI strip); T30 (readiness-gated roadmap); T31 (micro-market cascade); T32 (scoring and selection); T33 (business case page); T48 (benchmark case study playbook).
- __OS_DIR__/active-areas.md: the engagement's area, above all what the client and the partner rejected.
- __OS_DIR__/thinking-patterns.md: Challenge vs execution (initiative design is a challenge zone); Frameworks (test and control, readiness gates, adopt or adapt or avoid, scoring with robustness); Closed debates.

## Steps
1. Run T7 on each initiative in order: what specifically breaks today, read from raw sources; what a peer-standard fix leaves untouched; the design that goes past it; what better looks like in numbers.
2. Find the true gap and design the difference-maker; never stop at benchmark parity. Picks may change on the evidence: say which and why before building.
3. Write it as the client's own plan: start from what the client already runs, state as-is mechanics correctly, and sequence by capability so no step outruns readiness.
4. Select with T32: score against the fixed rubric, quote the weights and cut-off from T32 (never invent them), rerun the rule under several weightings, tag each pick robust or weight-sensitive, and put the rubric in the appendix.
5. Specify the pages: T22 for priority initiatives, T23 for the second wave, T24 for co-creation sessions, T26 and T27 for journeys and personas, T28 and T48 for benchmark cases, T29 for the KPI strip, T31 for the cascade, T33 for the business case.
6. Pilots run as test and control with a scale gate; the roadmap is gated on readiness, not months (T30).
7. Channel schemes carry the competition-law guardrails in output-standards.md: recommended maximum price only, no resale conditions, no price discussion with distributors.
8. Closed debates in thinking-patterns.md stay closed; a design never reopens one.

## Rules enforced here
- Our-play pages are never text-only: each carries a design table, a visual and the pilot with its control [A9].
- Less text, more visual; the design is pushed until each page would survive a top-tier partner review [A8].
- Scheme pages show the design (who qualifies, what unlocks the payout, how it steps up, where the money comes from, how it settles), never rates; rates read "set at pilot" and illustrative rates go to the appendix [A12].
- Change only the initiative slides asked for; every other slide stays untouched, even where the template suggests a change [A10].
- The agreed levers and house stay intact; for any structural change, think and tell first and change only once agreed [A14].
- Each initiative leads with what it addresses and what we are building, never a committed value number [A5].
- Numbers not yet built read "TBD, to size"; unconfirmed facts read "to validate"; co-creation pages carry no value or return numbers.
- Case studies show money invested, impact in absolute numbers and money, and the timeline (T28).
- Journey pages use the standard stage lexicon and an inputs placeholder row (T26), never invented labels.

## Delegate
- Benchmark, case and fact lookups (with source and date) to runner; scoring arithmetic and weighting reruns to runner, while the rubric judgement stays here.
- Data the business case needs to /data-request (T35 data request workbook), raised as the critical path.
- The agreed page content to /deck-build; an independent check to critic before anything leaves.

## Output
- Per initiative: the four T7 answers, the pick with its robustness tag, then page content in the T22 field spec (T23 or T24 where those apply), ready for /deck-build.
- In chat: picks that changed and why, structural proposals awaiting approval, and the data gaps that block sizing.
- Agreed decisions go to the engagement hub (T5 decision and action log).
- M1 no em dashes. M3 final deck pages only, no version language. M5 never invent a number, rate, case or quote. M6 name the file version you built on.
